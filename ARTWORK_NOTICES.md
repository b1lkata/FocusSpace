# Artwork sources and rights

The original UI includes artist/album artwork obtained from Apple/iTunes artwork URLs. Source URLs are recorded in src/renderer/music/collectionArtwork.json; the local resolution map is src/renderer/music/verifiedArtwork.json. Bundled JPEGs are presentation assets, not a grant of ownership or redistribution rights. Rights remain with the respective artists, labels and artwork owners; the project evaluation license does not license those images or provider recordings.

Artwork matching was checked for the curated references, but that does not certify the authorship or licensing of community-upload recordings. Independent provider thumbnails remain subject to provider metadata and terms. The neutral SVG fallback and sample-demo vector assets are original project assets.
